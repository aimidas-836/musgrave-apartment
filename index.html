About This Apartment

This 53m² senior apartment offers the perfect combination of comfort, security, and independence in the prestigious Musgrave area of Durban. Designed specifically with senior living in mind, this elegant residence provides a safe and welcoming environment for residents aged 55 and above.

The apartment features a thoughtfully designed layout including a welcoming entrance hall, open-plan living/dining area, modern kitchenette, spacious bedroom with air conditioning, and a full bathroom with senior-friendly safety features. The private balcony offers beautiful views of the tropical gardens and provides a peaceful retreat.

Located in a secure, gated complex with 24/7 security, CCTV monitoring, and controlled access. The building features elegant colonial-style architecture with white decorative balustrades, classical columns, and is surrounded by lush, well-maintained gardens with mature palm trees.

Healthcare and safety are paramount, with emergency call systems installed in both the bedroom and bathroom, on-site medical monitoring available, and immediate access to emergency services. The apartment includes accessibility features such as non-slip flooring, grab bars, and wide doorways.

The complex offers a vibrant community atmosphere with organized social activities, communal areas for residents to gather, and proximity to shopping centers, medical facilities, and all essential services in Musgrave. This is an exceptional opportunity for independent seniors seeking a comfortable, secure, and socially engaging lifestyle.
