About This Property

Welcome to this exquisite 53m² apartment nestled in the heart of prestigious Musgrave, one of Durban's most sought-after neighborhoods. This elegant residence combines classic colonial architecture with modern comfort, offering a unique living experience that's both sophisticated and secure.

The property features beautiful white colonial-style architecture with distinctive decorative balustrades and classical columns. The cross-pattern balcony railings provide both privacy and aesthetic appeal, while offering lovely views of the lush tropical landscaping below. Mature palm trees and well-maintained gardens create a serene, resort-like atmosphere.

Inside, you'll discover light-filled interiors with thoughtful design elements including large windows for natural ventilation and quality finishes throughout. The bedroom is equipped with air conditioning for year-round comfort and features direct access to the private balcony.

Security is paramount in this complex, with 24/7 secure access, gated parking, and well-lit communal areas. The property is perfect for professionals seeking a tranquil retreat, small families wanting a secure upscale environment, or investors looking for a prime Durban property with excellent rental potential.
